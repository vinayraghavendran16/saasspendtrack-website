Why this product exists

For most of the last decade, I watched finance and RevOps teams try to manage their SaaS stack with a Google Sheet. The sheet always starts the same way: vendor, cost, renewal date, owner, a notes column that fills up with half-finished questions. It works for a month. Then someone forgets to update it. Then a contract auto-renews that nobody wanted to keep. Then the CFO asks for a quarterly spend report and the sheet gets rebuilt from scratch.

The usual next step was a tool like Zylo, Vendr, or Spendesk. These are good products built for companies with 1,000+ employees and the procurement team to match. For the 20 to 500 person segment I spent most of my career in, they were priced wrong, too heavy to deploy, and focused on problems we didn't have yet.

Nobody was building what we actually needed: structured enough to replace the spreadsheet, light enough to deploy in an afternoon, priced like a tool instead of a platform. So I built it.

The architectural bet

SaaS Spend Track runs entirely in your browser. There is no backend. Every contract, vendor record, and spend number you enter stays on your device in localStorage. The only external calls are optional: a frankfurter.app currency rate fetch if you change display currency, an Apps Script endpoint that captures your signup email for the trial drip, and the Razorpay checkout page when you pay.

Three things follow from that choice:

  • Your data never leaves your machine. There is no vendor-side breach surface. Your compliance team gets a one-sentence answer to the data-residency question.
  • The pricing can stay flat. No per-user infrastructure cost on my side means no per-user pricing on yours. $3,499/year covers the tool, every feature, unlimited users on the same workspace.
  • There are real limits. No SSO (nothing to SSO into). No real-time shadow-SaaS discovery (no backend to connect to Okta). No team-wide centralised view across browsers (workspace is per-browser). These are deliberate trade-offs, not bugs. If any of them are deal-breakers, Zylo or Vendr is the right call, and I'll tell you so on the compare page.

What's live today

Shipping updates most weeks. Here's where the product is as of November 2026:

  • Ask Track - natural-language assistant with 20+ intents (renewals, spend, utilization, contracts, risk). No SQL, no filters, no dashboard hunting.
  • Tool discovery & bulk import - paste a CSV from any source, auto-map 60+ column aliases, fuzzy-match duplicates. Discover your SaaS stack in minutes.
  • Multi-currency - 9 currencies (USD, INR, EUR, GBP, AUD, SGD, AED, JPY, CAD) with live FX and per-subscription currency override.
  • Reports - Monthly Spend PDF, Renewal Roster CSV, Savings Opportunities PDF, all generated client-side.
  • Command palette - ⌘K brings up 33 indexed actions. Keyboard-first for anyone who lives in their browser.
  • Installable PWA - one-click install on iOS, Android, Windows, macOS, Linux. Home-screen icon, no app store round-trip.
  • Guided onboarding - Whatfix-style checklist, spotlight tour, beacons. New users get to value without a success call.
  • Diagnostics - in-app health check that shows module status, applied patches, state size, and runs the full test suite inline.
