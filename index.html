FAQ

Questions? Answers.

What is SaaSSpendTrack and who is it for?

SaaS Spend Track is a spend management and contract intelligence platform built for global mid-market companies (50–500 employees) managing ₹5Cr+ or $500K+ of SaaS. It unifies spend tracking, 8-dimension risk scoring, renewals, approvals, licence forecasting, and natural-language search (Ask Track) in one tool. Runs entirely in the browser, with on-prem deployment available for regulated industries.

How much does SaaSSpendTrack cost?

One plan: $3,499/year for global customers, or ₹3,00,000/year for Indian customers (18% GST added at invoice time). Both billed in INR via Razorpay at today's exchange rate. Includes 250 active contracts, unlimited users, and every feature. $100/year per 50 additional contracts above 250. On-prem deployment available at the same price for BFSI, healthcare and other regulated industries.

How is SaaSSpendTrack different from Zluri or Spendflo?

Zluri and Spendflo cost $30,000-$60,000/year and target US enterprises. SaaS Spend Track is $3,499/year flat for global customers (₹3,00,000 for India), goes live in minutes, and supports on-prem for regulated industries (BFSI, healthcare, public sector) that cannot send contracts to a US cloud.

How long does onboarding take?

Minutes. Spin up a workspace in your browser, load a seed dataset or import your own via CSV / OCR, configure renewal alerts, and your dashboard is live. No implementation consultant needed. Cloud deployment is instant; on-prem deployment follows the system requirements guide and takes 1–2 days with your IT team.

Does SaaS Spend Track offer on-prem deployment?

Yes. BFSI, healthcare, public sector and other regulated industries can run SaaS Spend Track entirely on their own infrastructure. Same price ($3,499/year, or ₹3,00,000 for India), same features. The on-prem system requirements guide is available on request. Contact us.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. The tool is free to try at app.saasspendtrack.com with no credit card. Load a seed dataset to explore, or import your own contracts and invoices. The 14-day trial gives you full access to every feature.

What integrations does SaaS Spend Track support?

44 data-source connectors across 5 phases: SSO (Okta, Entra ID, Google Workspace, JumpCloud), direct vendor APIs (Salesforce, Slack, Zoom, GitHub, Datadog, HubSpot and 18 more), Indian finance systems (Tally, Zoho Books, RazorpayX, QuickBooks, NetSuite), unified-API aggregators (Finch, Merge.dev, Josys), and a Chrome extension for shadow-IT discovery. Browser-only until the server tier ships, then live syncing enables.
