FAQ

Questions? Answers.

What is SaaSSpendTrack and who is it for?

SaaSSpendTrack is renewal-defense software for SaaS contracts. You upload your contracts, we extract the auto-renew clause and the notice period, and we alert you before the cancellation window closes- while you can still cancel, downgrade, or renegotiate. It's built for the finance or ops person at a 20–500 employee company who owns renewals without a procurement team behind them.

What is a notice period or cancellation window?

Most SaaS contracts renew automatically unless you give written notice a fixed number of days before the renewal date- commonly 30, 60, or 90. That gap is the notice period, and the time you have to act is the cancellation window. Miss it by a day and you're contractually committed to another full term, even if the tool has been unused for months.

How much does SaaSSpendTrack cost?

Renewal tracking is free forever, with no vendor limit- unlimited vendors, contracts, and users on the Free plan. Pro is $49/month or $499/year and adds automatic clause extraction, Slack alerts and escalation, renewal prep briefs, and invoice tracking. Enterprise is $149/month or $1,499/year and adds price benchmarking, risk scoring, SSO, and ERP integrations.

Why is the free plan unlimited instead of capped at a few vendors?

Because renewal defense only works if it's complete. Tracking 3 vendors out of 24 leaves 21 that can still auto-renew on you, and a tool that catches some of your renewals isn't meaningfully better than none. So every vendor is free to track. What you pay for is the intelligence on top: reading the clauses, routing the alerts, and preparing the negotiation.

How is this different from a calendar reminder or a spreadsheet?

A calendar reminder assumes someone already read the contract, found the notice period, did the date math correctly, and will still be in the role when it fires. We read the contract for you, calculate the deadline from the actual clause, escalate to a second owner if the first doesn't act, and survive the person who set it up leaving.

How is SaaSSpendTrack different from Zluri or Spendflo?

Zluri and Spendflo are full SaaS management suites aimed at 500+ employee companies, typically costing tens of thousands per year with a multi-week implementation. They do more than we do. We do one thing- stopping renewals you didn't intend to make- and we do it starting free, with no implementation, in about 30 minutes. If you need full app discovery, license reclamation, and procurement workflow, they're the better fit.

How long does setup take?

Around 30 minutes. Upload or link your contracts, confirm the renewal and notice dates we pull out, choose who gets alerted, and you're done. No implementation consultant, no sales call, no credit card.

What if I don't have the contract PDF for a vendor?

Add the vendor manually with just the renewal date and you still get the countdown and the alerts. Clause extraction needs the document; tracking doesn't. Most teams start with the contracts they can find and backfill the rest over time.

What integrations do you support?

Google Drive for contract uploads and Slack for renewal alerts and escalation on Pro. CLM and ERP integrations plus SSO on Enterprise. More are added regularly.

How do I get support?

Email support@saasspendtrack.com on any plan, including Free- we respond within hours, not days. Enterprise customers get a dedicated Slack channel.
