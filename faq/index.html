Pricing + payment

How much does SaaS Spend Track cost?

$3,499 per year flat. Includes every feature, unlimited users on the same workspace, and 250 active contracts. Beyond 250 contracts, pricing is $100/year per 50 additional contracts.

How do I cancel?

There is no subscription to cancel in the traditional sense. The $3,499 is an annual charge via Razorpay; stop paying and the paid features gate at the end of your paid period.

Do you offer refunds?

Within 30 days of payment, if you write to hello@saasspendtrack.com and tell us why the tool didn't fit, we refund in full. After 30 days, case-by-case.

Is there a free tier?

You can start with a 7-day free trial. Continued access to paid features requires the annual plan.

Trial + signup

Is there a 7-day free trial?

Yes. Try it free for 7 days with a work email. No credit card required.

What email address can I sign up with?

Data + security

Where does my data live?

For information about workspace data and privacy, see our Privacy Policy.

What happens if SaaS Spend Track shuts down?

The last build you downloaded will keep running in your browser indefinitely. Your data is in your localStorage, not on our server. You can export the full workspace as JSON at any time from Settings.

Are you SOC 2 Type II certified?

SaaS Spend Track does not currently provide a SOC 2 Type II report. If SOC 2 is a procurement blocker regardless of architecture, we are not the right tool - Zylo and Vendr are SOC 2 Type II.

Do you support SSO / SAML?

Contact us to confirm the sign-in options supported for your workspace.

Is my data encrypted?

localStorage itself is not encrypted at rest by the browser. Full-disk encryption on your laptop (FileVault on Mac, BitLocker on Windows) handles that. We never transmit your data, so encryption-in-transit isn't a question that applies.

Features + integrations

What integrations do you support?

CSV import and export for every major data type (subscriptions, contracts, invoices, employees). PDF and DOCX parsing for uploaded contracts. Bulk import maps 60+ header aliases automatically. We do not have live API integrations with Okta, HRIS, accounting, or ERP systems - that requires a backend we don't have.

Can I share a workspace with my team?

Partial. Workspace is per-browser; opening the tool on a colleague's device gives them a fresh workspace. You can share by exporting your workspace as JSON and having them import it. True real-time multi-user is a future feature that would require a backend.

What currencies are supported?

Display in USD, EUR, GBP, AUD, SGD, AED, JPY, CAD. Each subscription can be priced in its own source currency; dashboards convert through USD using live rates from frankfurter.app.

Can I parse contracts automatically?

Yes. Upload a PDF, DOCX, or paste contract text. The parser extracts renewal date, notice period, auto-renew flag, price cap, term length, and scores the contract across 8 risk dimensions.

Can I generate reports?

Three built-in exports: Monthly Spend Summary PDF (headline numbers, top vendors, category breakdown, upcoming renewals), Renewal Roster CSV (12 months), Savings Opportunities PDF (underused licences, auto-renew traps, overlap, orphans - with $ estimates).

Is there an Ask Track NLP assistant?

Yes. Ask Track understands 20+ intents including "what renews this month", "show licences under 50% used", "total spend by department", "flag auto-renewing contracts in 30 days". Natural language in, filtered views out. Runs entirely in your browser - no training data leaves your device.

Is there a command palette?

Yes. Hit ⌘K (or Ctrl+K on Windows) to open a fuzzy launcher with 33 indexed actions: jump to any view, run any report, open Settings, or fire Ask Track with the ask: prefix.

Can I install it as an app on my phone or desktop?

Yes. SaaS Spend Track is a progressive web app (PWA). Installable on iOS (Share → Add to Home Screen), Android (install prompt in Chrome), and desktop (install prompt in Chrome/Edge). Home-screen icon, opens like a native app, works offline for views you've already opened. No app store, no update process.

How does tool discovery work?

Bulk CSV import is the discovery engine. Paste a list from any source - a spreadsheet, a bank statement, your SSO admin export, your old SaaS management tool - and the parser auto-maps 60+ column aliases (name, cost, renewal date, owner, category, etc.) and fuzzy-matches against your existing vendors so duplicates don't slip in. 50-vendor companies usually finish discovery in under 30 minutes.

Migration + alternatives

How do I migrate from Vendr / Zylo / Spendesk?

Export your vendor and contract data from your existing tool as CSV. Bulk import auto-detects 60+ header aliases. Most migrations take under 30 minutes for a 50-vendor company.

How is SaaS Spend Track different from Vendr?

Vendr sells human negotiators ($20-60k/yr); SaaS Spend Track sells the data your negotiators need ($3.5k/yr flat). Full comparison.

How is SaaS Spend Track different from Zylo?

Zylo is built for Fortune 1000 ($25-150k/yr). SaaS Spend Track is built for 20-500 person teams. Different scale, different price, different problem. Full comparison.

Do you support on-premise deployment?

Yes. Since the tool is a single HTML file running in a browser, on-prem means serving that file from your own infrastructure. Email hello@saasspendtrack.com for the deployment package.
