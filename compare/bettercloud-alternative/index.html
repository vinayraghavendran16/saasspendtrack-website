BetterCloud alternative: SaaS Spend Track for contract + spend without the IT automation surface

BetterCloud is for IT teams who automate SaaS operations. We're for finance and ops teams who just want the contract and spend piece.

Quick answer

BetterCloud is an IT-ops platform: SaaS discovery, user provisioning + deprovisioning, policy enforcement, workflow automation across 50+ connected SaaS tools. Priced for IT functions at $15k-$75k/year. SaaS Spend Track at $3,499/year flat focuses on contract + spend management only; no IT automation. Pick BetterCloud if your IT team runs SaaS operations as a function. Pick SaaS Spend Track if you're in finance or ops and you just need to track contracts and renewals.

Different buyer, different tool

BetterCloud's buyer is the IT director or CTO's office. The pitch: SaaS tools create a sprawling access-management problem. Employees join, change roles, leave; permissions need to stay current across 50+ apps. BetterCloud automates that via SCIM provisioning and policy workflows. Contract and spend management is a module, not the main event.

SaaS Spend Track's buyer is the CFO, VP Finance, head of procurement, or RevOps lead. The pitch: SaaS spend is scattered across spreadsheets, renewals get missed, and nobody has a single source of truth for what the company actually pays for. We do that core job well, at $3,499/year flat, without trying to replace your IT automation stack.

When BetterCloud wins

  • Your IT team manages SaaS ops as an operational function with dedicated headcount.
  • You need automated user deprovisioning across 20+ SaaS tools when employees leave.
  • You enforce security policies at scale: auto-revoke admin access after inactivity, require MFA on all admin accounts, etc.
  • Your company has 500+ employees and SaaS sprawl is a measurable IT problem.
  • Budget is in the $15k-$75k/year range for the IT management stack.

When SaaS Spend Track wins

  • The buyer is finance, procurement, or RevOps - not IT.
  • Your core problem is contract tracking, renewal visibility, and spend control - not user provisioning.
  • You have 20-500 employees, 10-80 vendors, and the IT automation piece isn't your pain point yet.
  • Budget is in the low thousands, not low tens of thousands.

Can you use both?

Yes. BetterCloud for IT-side automation (provisioning, access policies, offboarding workflows). SaaS Spend Track for finance-side contract and spend management. Different teams, different tools, no overlap conflict.

Frequently asked

What is a cheaper alternative to BetterCloud?

SaaS Spend Track at $3,499/year flat. BetterCloud typically prices $15k-$75k/year. Tradeoff: BetterCloud's core is IT automation (provisioning, deprovisioning, policy enforcement); SaaS Spend Track doesn't do that.

When is BetterCloud worth the price?

When IT manages SaaS as an operational function, needs SCIM provisioning across 50+ tools, enforces security policies at scale, and your company has 500+ employees with real automation ROI.

Does SaaS Spend Track automate IT workflows?

No. Those require API integration with your SaaS tools; We stay specialized on contract + spend.

Can SaaS Spend Track handle SaaS discovery like BetterCloud?

Not automatically via SSO. You can import invoice data via CSV and flag vendors not on your approved list. Suitable for mid-market; not a BetterCloud replacement at enterprise scale.
