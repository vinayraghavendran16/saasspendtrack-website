Productiv alternative: SaaS Spend Track for teams who don't need engagement-based usage intelligence

Productiv tracks what features you actually use. That's enterprise-grade. If you just need to know who's paying for what and when it renews, pay less.

Quick answer

Productiv's differentiator is feature-level engagement tracking: not just whether users log in, but which features they touch, how long they stay, which workflows they complete. Built for enterprise ($30k-$150k+/yr) where feature-level data drives large renewal negotiations. SaaS Spend Track at $3,499/yr tracks what you tell it (seats, utilization %, contracts) which is enough for 20-500 person teams. Pick Productiv for Fortune 1000 usage intelligence; pick SaaS Spend Track for mid-market contract + spend without the telemetry overhead.

What Productiv is actually for

Productiv's edge is depth of usage data. Through API integrations with 300+ SaaS tools, it pulls engagement signal at the feature level: for Salesforce, who's actually using Lightning vs Classic; for Zoom, meeting minutes per user per month; for Figma, whether editor seats are editing or just viewing. That data then drives their renewal benchmarking engine (they have anonymized usage data across hundreds of enterprise customers).

At enterprise scale this is genuinely valuable. If you spend $5M/yr on SaaS, a data-driven renewal negotiation can save $500k. Productiv's $50k-100k/yr fee pays for itself many times over.

At mid-market scale it's overkill. If you spend $400k/yr on SaaS, the same 10% savings is $40k. Productiv's fee eats most of it. You need lighter infrastructure.

What SaaS Spend Track is for

We track what you tell us. Seat count, assigned seats, utilized seats, contract terms, renewal dates. You update utilization quarterly (manually or via CSV import from your SaaS tool's native usage export). That's less sophisticated than Productiv's live feature-level telemetry, and for teams with 30-80 vendors it's enough to drive meaningful right-sizing conversations at renewal.

When Productiv wins

  • 1,000+ employees with real SaaS spend ($2M+/yr) where feature-level data meaningfully changes negotiation outcomes.
  • You need benchmark data on what other enterprises pay for the same SaaS tools — Productiv has it.
  • You have a dedicated SaaS management function (someone whose job this is).
  • Budget is in the $30k-$150k+/year range and the ROI math pencils out at that spend scale.

When SaaS Spend Track wins

  • Under 500 employees. Feature-level telemetry depth doesn't pay back at your scale.
  • You have a sense of which seats are underused. Quarterly manual updates are workable; you don't need continuous live data.
  • Your budget is in the low thousands, not low tens of thousands.
  • You care enough about data privacy that "browser-only, nothing leaves this device" is an actual advantage.
  • You want to try the tool today without a sales call, procurement review, or weeks of onboarding.
