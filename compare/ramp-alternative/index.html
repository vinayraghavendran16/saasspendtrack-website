Ramp alternative: SaaS Spend Track for teams who want contract depth, not card + spend bundling

Ramp is a cards + expenses + bill-pay platform that added SaaS management. If you only want the SaaS piece done well, pay for the specialist.

Quick answer

Ramp is a corporate card and expense management platform. SaaS management is one of several modules built on top of the card transaction data. It's free for the core product (Ramp makes money on interchange from your corporate card usage). SaaS Spend Track at $3,499/yr is a dedicated SaaS contract and spend specialist with deeper parsing, risk scoring, and renewal management. Pick Ramp if you want cards + expenses + light SaaS tracking in one platform. Pick SaaS Spend Track if you already have cards figured out and want serious contract management.

Not quite the same category

Ramp's business is corporate cards and spend management. SaaS management entered the product because once you're issuing cards and tracking expense data, you see SaaS subscriptions flow through the transaction stream. Flagging duplicates and tracking renewal dates is a natural adjacent feature. For many teams it's "good enough" and the price is unbeatable: free, because Ramp's revenue comes from interchange on the card spend you route through them.

SaaS Spend Track doesn't do cards. We don't do expense management. We don't do bill pay. We do SaaS contract and spend management specifically, at the depth a dedicated tool can reach: PDF contract parsing, 8-dimension risk scoring, 12-month licence forecasting, natural-language queries over your contract corpus.

The "free" question

Ramp's SaaS module is free. So is their core card product. The business model is interchange - Ramp earns ~1% of your card spend as a merchant fee from the card networks. For a US-based company running $5M of card spend through Ramp annually, they earn $50k from your business without charging you a cent. That's a legitimate value exchange.

Two things worth noting if you're weighing Ramp against a paid dedicated tool:

  • "Free" aligns Ramp's incentives with routing more spend through their cards, not with giving you the best possible SaaS management experience. Feature depth in the SaaS module won't be their first priority.
  • Ramp's SaaS module is US-centric. If you're outside the US or not using Ramp cards, the automated discovery piece doesn't work and the manual experience is basic.

When Ramp wins

  • You're a US-based company that wants one platform for corporate cards, expenses, bill pay, and light SaaS management.
  • You already route significant spend through Ramp cards and the auto-discovery of subscriptions from card data is genuinely useful.
  • Your SaaS management needs are basic: track what you pay, flag duplicates, remember renewal dates.
  • Budget-conscious: Ramp is free, SaaS Spend Track is $3,499/yr.

When SaaS Spend Track wins

  • You're outside the US (India, APAC, Europe) and Ramp's card product doesn't apply to you.
  • You already have cards + expenses figured out (Brex, Pleo, your bank, an Indian equivalent).
  • Your SaaS management pain is specifically contract depth: parsing clauses, risk scoring, renewal negotiations.
  • You want multi-currency display and per-vendor source currency (we support multiple currencies).

Can you use both?

Yes, and many US teams do. Ramp for cards + expenses + bill pay. SaaS Spend Track for serious contract management on top. Export invoice data from Ramp as CSV and bulk-import into SaaS Spend Track to reconcile.
