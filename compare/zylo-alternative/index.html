Zylo alternative: SaaS Spend Track for mid-market teams who don't need enterprise SMP

Zylo is built for Fortune 1000. If you're 20-500 people, you probably shouldn't buy it. Here's what you should buy instead.

Quick answer

Zylo is a mature enterprise SaaS management platform priced from ~$25k to $150k+/year depending on headcount and integrations. SaaS Spend Track is $3,499/year flat with a narrower feature set aimed at the 20-500 employee segment. If you have 1,000+ employees and 100+ vendors and need auto-discovery via SSO, pick Zylo. If you have 20-500 employees and 10-80 vendors you already know about, Zylo is overkill.

What Zylo is actually for

Zylo is a comprehensive enterprise SaaS management platform. Its core value proposition is automated discovery: connect it to Okta, your HRIS, your expense system, and your accounting data, and it surfaces every SaaS tool your employees are using, including the ones IT doesn't know about. That's genuinely useful when you have 2,000 employees across 15 business units and the finance team has no idea what's actually running.

The architecture required to do that (API integrations, data pipelines, SSO parsing, employee-to-app mapping, usage tracking) is also what makes it expensive and slow to deploy. You're paying for the discovery engine, whether or not you need it.

What SaaS Spend Track is actually for

SaaS Spend Track assumes you already have a list of your SaaS tools. Maybe it's in a Google Sheet. Maybe it's in your head. The product takes that list, adds structure (renewal dates, notice periods, owners, contracts, usage), and gives you dashboards + exports + a natural-language query layer on top. It does not discover shadow SaaS automatically because it has no backend to connect to Okta.

For a 50-person company with 25 subscriptions, "discovery" takes 30 minutes with a CSV. You don't need an automated engine to find them.

When Zylo is the right call

  • Headcount over 1,000. The discovery automation genuinely saves real headcount-equivalent work.
  • Over 100 SaaS tools in active use, many owned by business-unit heads instead of central IT.
  • You need benchmark data on what other companies pay for the same vendor. Zylo's Discovery product has unique data on this.
  • SOC 2 Type II and SSO are hard compliance requirements.
  • Your procurement or finance team has budget to buy a $50k+ platform and the operational capacity to onboard it.

When SaaS Spend Track is the right call

  • Headcount 20-500. The discovery feature Zylo charges premium for doesn't move the needle at this scale.
  • You have 10-80 active SaaS vendors. You know what they are. The problem isn't finding them, it's managing them.
  • Your budget for a SaaS management tool is in the low thousands, not low hundred-thousands.
  • You care enough about data location that "runs in your browser, nothing leaves this device" is an actual advantage, not just a marketing line.
  • You want to be using the tool today, not twelve weeks from now.

Who should NOT pick SaaS Spend Track

  • Teams where discovering unknown-unknown SaaS is the primary problem. Zylo and BetterCloud are better here.
  • Compliance-first environments that require SOC 2 Type II of every vendor.
  • Companies that want a vendor to negotiate contracts on their behalf. See Vendr for that.

Frequently asked

What is a cheaper alternative to Zylo?

SaaS Spend Track at $3,499/year flat is a mid-market alternative to Zylo, which typically prices in the $25k-$150k/year range depending on employee count and integrations. The tradeoff is that SaaS Spend Track doesn't do automated SaaS discovery via SSO or API; you add vendors manually or via bulk CSV import.

When is Zylo worth the price?

Zylo makes financial sense when your organization has 1,000+ employees, 100+ SaaS vendors, a dedicated IT / Finance team managing them, and regulatory needs for SOC 2 Type II, SAML SSO, and per-user audit logs. Below that threshold, the integration complexity and cost outweigh the automation benefit.

Can SaaS Spend Track discover shadow SaaS automatically?

Not from SSO or expense data directly; the tool runs entirely in your browser so it has no API access to Okta, your accounting system, or your corporate cards. It identifies likely shadow SaaS by letting you import invoice data via CSV and flagging vendor names that don't match your approved subscription list. This is manual-import-based discovery, not real-time.
