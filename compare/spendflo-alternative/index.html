Spendflo alternative: SaaS Spend Track for Indian teams that want software, not services

Spendflo includes human buyers in the price. If you want to negotiate your own renewals with better data, pay less.

Quick answer

Spendflo is a SaaS procurement-as-a-service company built in India, offering software plus human buyers who negotiate on your behalf. SaaS Spend Track is software only at $3,499/yr. Pick Spendflo if you want outsourced procurement. Pick SaaS Spend Track if you have someone who can negotiate internally with the right data, and you'd rather pay for the data than the buyers.

Same country, different models

Both Spendflo and SaaS Spend Track are built in India for Indian + global customers. Both target teams that have outgrown spreadsheets. The core difference is the service layer.

Spendflo bundles people with software. You get the SaaS management dashboard plus access to a buyer who negotiates renewals for you, benchmarks your vendor contracts against their database of similar deals, and often absorbs some of the contract-management overhead. Request a quote for the service scope you need.

SaaS Spend Track is software only. You get the dashboard, contract parser, risk engine, forecasting, and exports. You run the vendor negotiations yourself using the data the tool surfaces. Priced flat at $3,499/yr.

When Spendflo wins

  • You want Spendflo's benchmark data on what other Indian mid-market companies pay for the same vendors. They have it; nobody else at this price point does.
  • Your finance leadership wants a service relationship with KPIs, not a tool to operate.

When SaaS Spend Track wins

  • You have someone in finance or ops who can run vendor conversations with the right data. Every renewal you don't need to escalate is pure margin.
  • You want to try it today without a sales call, procurement review, or 2-4 week onboarding. Sign up with your work email; see your dashboard in 10 minutes.
  • Your contract data being on a vendor's AWS cloud (even in Mumbai) is a compliance conversation you'd rather not have.
  • You're a 20-100 person team where Spendflo's engagement floor is above your total SaaS spend.
