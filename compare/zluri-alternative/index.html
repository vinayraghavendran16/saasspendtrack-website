Zluri alternative: SaaS Spend Track for teams priced out of enterprise SaaS management

Same country, different scale. Zluri is building a platform. We're building a tool.

Quick answer

Zluri is a comprehensive SaaS management platform (discovery + access management + procurement + finance integrations) priced $10k-$40k/yr. SaaS Spend Track is a focused SaaS management tool (contracts + spend + risk) priced $2,000/yr. Pick Zluri if you need access management and auto-discovery across 300+ employees. Pick SaaS Spend Track if you have 20-500 employees, 10-80 vendors, and want to replace your spreadsheet without buying a platform.

Same country, different ambition

Both Zluri and SaaS Spend Track are built in Bengaluru. Both serve the same broad market of companies that have outgrown spreadsheets. The difference is scope and pricing philosophy.

Zluri is building the SaaS management platform. Their product covers discovery (auto-finding SaaS via SSO, browser extensions, finance data), access management (provisioning + deprovisioning via SCIM), vendor management (contracts, renewals, risk), and procurement (requests, approvals, purchasing). It's a Series B company with a product roadmap that keeps expanding the platform surface.

SaaS Spend Track is building a focused tool. We do contracts, spend, risk, forecasting, exports. We don't do discovery or access management and problems that only exist at larger scale. Everything else - the product design, the $2,000 price, the 10-minute deploy - follows from that scope choice.

When Zluri wins

  • Headcount 300+ with departments or business units purchasing SaaS independently.
  • You need SCIM-based access management - the ability to provision and deprovision users across 50+ SaaS tools automatically when someone joins, changes role, or leaves.
  • You want procurement workflows: new-tool requests routed through finance approval, vendor security reviews, budget check.
  • You need SOC 2 Type II and SSO as hard compliance requirements.
  • Your IT team is managing the SaaS stack as an ongoing operational function, not a quarterly review.

When SaaS Spend Track wins

  • Headcount 20-500. The access-management and discovery features Zluri charges for don't move the needle at this scale.
  • You have 10-80 known SaaS vendors. The problem isn't finding them; it's managing contracts, renewals, and forecasting spend.
  • Your budget for this tool is thousands, not tens of thousands.
  • You want to try the tool today without a sales call, procurement review, or weeks of onboarding.

Migration

If you're already on Zluri and want to downshift to SaaS Spend Track because the platform is too much for your current stage, export your vendor and contract data as CSV. SaaS Spend Track's bulk import auto-detects 60+ common header aliases (including Zluri's default exports). Most migrations take under 30 minutes for a 50-vendor company.

What you'll lose: automated discovery, access-management automation, procurement workflow. What you'll gain: lower annual cost, faster workflow for the parts you actually use daily, and your data in your own browser instead of on a vendor's cloud.

Frequently asked

What is the cheapest alternative to Zluri?

SaaS Spend Track at $2,000/year. Zluri typically prices $10k-$40k/year depending on employee count, integrations, and modules. Tradeoff: SaaS Spend Track focuses on contract + spend management only; does not auto-discover or do access management.

When is Zluri worth the price?

When your company has 300+ employees across multiple business units, needs automated SaaS discovery, requires access-management workflows (provision/deprovision), and your IT team needs centralized visibility across the full SaaS lifecycle.

Is SaaS Spend Track built in India like Zluri?

Yes, both are Bengaluru-based. Zluri is a larger Series B team; SaaS Spend Track is a focused single-founder product priced accordingly.

Can I migrate from Zluri to SaaS Spend Track?

Yes. Export your vendor and contract data from Zluri as CSV, use SaaS Spend Track's bulk import. Under 30 minutes for a 50-vendor migration. You lose Zluri's discovery and access-management features; you gain lower cost and faster workflow.
