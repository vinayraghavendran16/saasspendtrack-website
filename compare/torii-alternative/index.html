Torii alternative: SaaS Spend Track for teams who don't need automated SaaS discovery

Torii is a discovery engine wrapped in a management platform. If you know your stack already, discovery is the expensive part you're not using.

Quick answer

Torii's defining feature is automated SaaS discovery - connect it to Okta, your HRIS, your expense system, install a browser extension, and it surfaces every SaaS tool employees use, including ones IT doesn't know about. Priced at $15k-$50k/year accordingly. SaaS Spend Track at $3,499/year assumes you already have a list of your SaaS tools and focuses on managing them well. Pick Torii if shadow SaaS discovery is your actual problem; pick SaaS Spend Track if you know what you're paying for and want to manage it better.

What Torii is actually for

Torii's SSO + browser extension + finance integration combo gives it a privileged view across your organization that few other tools can match. It sees what Okta sees. It sees what credit card expense data reveals. It sees what employees are logging into from their browsers. That combination produces a near-complete picture of SaaS usage - including the shadow stuff that never touched IT's radar.

That's genuinely useful at 500+ employee scale where SaaS sprawl is real. It's expensive because building and maintaining those integrations costs money.

What SaaS Spend Track is for

SaaS Spend Track assumes you already have a vendor list. Maybe it's a Google Sheet. Maybe it's in your head. Bulk-import it in five minutes with CSV, add contracts, and the tool handles the renewal calendar, risk scoring, forecasting, and exports. We don't do SSO-based discovery because we have no backend to connect to Okta.

For a 60-person company with 25 vendors, discovery is a 30-minute spreadsheet exercise. You don't need a $30k automated engine for that; you need help managing the 25 vendors you already have.

When Torii wins

  • 500+ employees with decentralized SaaS purchasing. Shadow SaaS is a real problem; you need the discovery automation.
  • You want automated user offboarding - when someone leaves the company, deactivate them in every SaaS tool automatically.
  • Your IT team is already managing SSO via Okta and wants a complementary SaaS management layer that plugs into it.
  • Budget is in the $15k+/year range and you have procurement capacity to onboard a mid-complexity platform.

When SaaS Spend Track wins

  • Under 500 employees. Discovery is a one-time 30-minute exercise, not a $30k/year automated problem.
  • You already have a list of your SaaS tools; what you don't have is a good way to track contracts, renewals, and risk.
  • Your budget for this tool is in the low thousands, not low tens of thousands.
  • You want to be using the tool today, not six weeks from now.

Frequently asked

What is a cheaper alternative to Torii?

SaaS Spend Track at $3,499/year flat. Torii typically prices in the $15k-$50k/year range depending on employee count and integrations. Tradeoff: SaaS Spend Track does not auto-discover SaaS.

When is Torii worth the price?

When your company has 500+ employees, departments purchasing independently, no central IT oversight of SaaS procurement, and real shadow-SaaS risk. Torii's automated discovery saves actual headcount work at that scale.

Can SaaS Spend Track discover shadow SaaS?

Not automatically via SSO. You can import invoice data via CSV and the tool flags vendors not on your approved list. Suitable for small/mid teams; not a substitute for Torii's automated engine at enterprise scale.

Does SaaS Spend Track automate SaaS offboarding?

No. Torii does this via API integration with your HRIS and SaaS tools. SaaS Spend Track has no backend for such integrations and stays specialized on contract + spend.
