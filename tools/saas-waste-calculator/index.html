How this calculator works

The total combines the unused-license and overlapping-tool estimates. Missed-renewal exposure uses the count you entered for the past two years and appears separately.

  • Unused licenses - Your monthly spend × 12 × (100% minus your utilization input). If you spend $15k/month at 75% utilization, that's $45,000/year on seats nobody opens.
  • Overlapping tools - Number of overlapping tools × 70% of your average cost per tool. The 70% factor is an illustrative assumption.
  • Auto-renewal exposure - Missed notice windows × average annual contract value × a 40% risk weight. The 40% factor is an illustrative risk weight.

For a budget decision, use actual contract prices, seat usage, and signed renewal terms.

Frequently asked

How is SaaS waste calculated?

The total estimates unused-license and overlapping-tool costs from your inputs. Missed-renewal exposure is shown separately.

What is an acceptable SaaS utilization rate?

Use paid-seat usage over a representative period to identify subscriptions to review. Check seasonal use and contract minimums before reducing seats.

How often should I audit my SaaS stack?

Quarterly for fast-growing teams, twice a year for stable ones. The audit should cover active utilization (who is using what), contract status (what renews when), and overlap (which tools cover the same job).

What is a realistic savings target?

Set a target after verifying usage, redundant tools, and contract terms. Count only changes your team can make before the relevant renewal or notice deadline.
