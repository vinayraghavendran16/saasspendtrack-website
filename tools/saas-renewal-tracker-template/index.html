What to track for each SaaS contract

Add one row per agreement in Excel or Google Sheets. Record the renewal date, notice period, cancel-by date, annual cost, owner, and renewal decision.

Renewal date
The date the next term begins.
Cancel-by date
The last date to send notice under the signed agreement.
Owner
The person responsible for confirming the terms and getting a decision.

Calculate the notice deadline

When the agreement uses calendar days, subtract the notice period from its renewal date. June 30 minus 60 calendar days is May 1. Check the agreement for its notice delivery requirements and verify the date before relying on it.
