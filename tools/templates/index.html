Why we made these free

Every template on this page is something we built for internal use when SaaS Spend Track didn't exist. Giving them away costs us nothing. If you use them for a year and never need more, great - you saved some money and we spent 30 minutes putting the file together. If you use them for a month and realise you need the tool version with parsing, risk scoring, and renewal alerts baked in, the trial is here.

All files are plain CSV. Open in Google Sheets, Excel, Numbers, or any text editor. We don't gate them with email because we think that's a cheap trick; if the templates are useful, you'll come find the product on your own.
