SaaS Vendor Governance

Give finance, IT, procurement, and legal a shared vendor register for owners, approvals, and review dates.

What is a SaaS vendor register?

A vendor register records each software supplier, its business owner, contract status, and review history. It gives teams a place to check who is responsible for the next decision.

Track approvals in one place

Record finance, procurement, legal, and information-security approval statuses. Assign a reviewer and set the next review date before a contract comes up for renewal.

Keep risk reviews actionable

Record the issue, who owns it, and what has to happen before approval. Use the register to prepare renewals and follow up on outstanding reviews.
