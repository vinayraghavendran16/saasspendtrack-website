Can I track the notice deadline separately from the renewal date?

Yes. The renewal view shows the notice deadline alongside the renewal date and annual cost, so you can see when a decision needs to happen.

What is a SaaS cancellation deadline?

It is the last date to give notice under the contract. If a contract renews June 30 and requires 60 calendar days’ notice, May 1 is the date to verify against the signed terms.

Build a renewal calendar

Record the renewal date, notice period, auto-renew clause, contract owner, and expected cost. Review the deadlines that fall in the next 30, 60, and 90 days.

Give each renewal a decision

Assign an owner to check usage, confirm the terms, and choose whether to renew, downgrade, renegotiate, or cancel. Save the notice and any vendor acknowledgment with the record.
