SaaS Contract Management

Organize software agreements and review renewal clauses, notice periods, and vendor risk before committing to another term.

What belongs in a SaaS contract register?

Keep the signed agreement, vendor, owner, term, annual value, renewal date, notice period, and auto-renew status together. Add amendments or order forms that change the original terms.

Review extracted terms before acting

Use document intake to help find the renewal clause, pricing, notice terms, and price caps. Verify extracted terms against the signed agreement before sending notices or making a purchase decision.

Compare options before the deadline

Use the contract record to assess the cost of renewal, seat reductions, or switching. Document the decision and assign an owner for follow-through.
